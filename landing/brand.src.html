Guidelines

Using the logo

✓ Do

Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the icon mark's height. Use the dark version on dark-colored backgrounds/images. Preserve the aspect ratio when resizing.

✕ Don't

Don't change the gradient colors, don't stretch/distort it, don't add drop shadows or other effects, and don't place it on low-contrast backgrounds.
